TapRace Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Summary

TapRace has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your information stays on your phone, or goes only to the players in the same room as you.

This policy explains what the app stores, what it shares with nearby players, and why it asks for Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Location permissions.

What the app stores on your device

TapRace saves a few things in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your nickname and chosen avatar
  • Your language setting (English or Ukrainian)
  • A leaderboard: nickname, avatar, rating, wins, games played and best reaction time for players you have played with

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account.

What is shared with nearby players

When you create or join a room, the app sends the following directly to the other phones in that room, over Google's Nearby Connections:

  • Your nickname and avatar
  • The room PIN, if the room has one (the host checks it)
  • Your vote for the next game, and your game results, such as reaction times
  • Your copy of the leaderboard, so everyone sees the same ratings

The room name, the host's nickname and whether the room has a PIN, and the room size are visible to anyone nearby who is looking for rooms. Other players in your room can see your nickname, avatar and ratings. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as a nickname. The room's game settings (the level, whether players vote for the next game, and what a mistake costs) are also shared with everyone in the room.

Permissions

The app asks for these Android permissions, only so phones can find and connect to each other:

  • Bluetooth (scan, advertise, connect): to discover rooms and connect to the host.
  • Wi-Fi state and Nearby Wi-Fi devices: to move to a faster Wi-Fi link once connected.
  • Location (precise and approximate): Android requires this permission for nearby device discovery on many versions. TapRace does not read, store or send your location.

Two games use your phone's hardware, which needs no permission. The Shake and Pose games read the motion sensor (accelerometer) while you play, to detect shaking and how the phone is held. These readings are used only on your phone during the game and are never stored or sent. The Sound game plays short sounds through your speaker; TapRace does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

If you refuse these permissions, you can still open the app and view the leaderboard, but you cannot create or join a room.

Third parties

Nearby connections run through Nearby Connections, part of Google Play services. Google may process technical data for this feature under Google's Privacy Policy.

TapRace contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Children

TapRace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because players in a room can see each other's nicknames, parents should help young players choose a nickname that does not identify them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you remove it. To delete it, open Android Settings, then Apps, then TapRace, then Storage, and choose Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Players you have played with may keep your nickname, avatar and results on their own phones. We cannot delete data from other people's phones.

Security

Phones connect directly to each other, and no data passes through a server that we run. The room PIN is a simple code to keep nearby strangers out of a friendly game, not strong protection. Do not use a PIN that you use for anything else.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting or sharing anything new, we will update this policy and its "Last updated" date before the change goes live.
